THE REAL PROBLEM

On social media, the best product doesn't win. The one that stops the scroll first does.

Most businesses post content because 'you have to post'. White background, product photo, price. No identity, no narrative, no reason for anyone to stop. The result: ad budget wasted on content nobody remembers.

You can invest in paid ads, but if the content doesn't stop the scroll, the money is gone. Design is what turns investment into results.

The work with Orbidental and Biotec combined deliberate design with strategic paid ads: pieces that earn attention visually and investment that puts them in front of the right people. The results aren't a coincidence.
