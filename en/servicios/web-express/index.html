FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

The questions everyone asks

What if I need more than 5 pages?
No problem: each additional page is quoted before we start (usually between $80 and $120 depending on complexity). Charges never appear mid-project.
When does the 14-day clock start?
From the moment I receive your complete content: copy, photos and logo. If you do not have it yet, I give you a guide to prepare it and we set the date once it is ready — so the promise is real, not fine print.
How do I pay from the US, Europe or LATAM?
In USD via international transfer, Wise or PayPal (local transfer inside Colombia). 50% to book your date and 50% once the site is published and working.
Do I own the site?
Yes, all of it: domain, hosting and code stay in your accounts and under your ownership. I hand over the credentials and a short guide for basic edits, so you never depend on me.
Does it include ranking on Google?
It includes the full technical foundation: structure, metadata, schema, sitemap and speed. Sustained ranking is a different job — the SEO + GEO Audit tells you what to do, and the Monthly plan executes it.
