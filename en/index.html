FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Things people
always ask.

Clear answers so you can make confident decisions and we can work with full transparency.

01 Do you work with clients outside Colombia?

Yes — I work 100% remotely with clients in several countries. All communication is written — WhatsApp or email — so every agreement, update and delivery stays documented. I'm in Bogotá (UTC-5) and I adapt to your time zone.

02 How does payment work?

I work with a 50% deposit to kick things off and the remaining 50% on delivery. For larger projects we split it into milestones. For international clients I accept PayPal or Wise.

03 How many revisions are included?

It depends on the service, but generally I include two rounds of revisions within the agreed scope. If you need more after that, we price it separately — no surprises. What matters is that the final result is right.

04 What time zone do you work in?

I'm in Bogotá, Colombia — UTC-5, no daylight saving. I'm usually available between 8am and 8pm Colombian time, but I'm flexible when we need to schedule a call across time zones.

05 Do you use contracts?

Always. Before starting any project we sign an agreement that defines the scope, timeline, price, and delivery terms. It protects both of us and prevents misunderstandings down the road.

06 What if I need changes after delivery?

Minor changes within the original scope are covered at no extra cost if you request them within the first 7 days. After that, or for significant direction changes, we price them separately — always with a clear quote before touching anything.

07 How much does a project cost?

Projects start at $500 USD for an initial branding or basic-website scope; most land between $500 and $2,500 USD depending on scope. Before we start you get a proposal with a fixed price, timeline and deliverables — no surprises, no hidden costs.

08 What happens if you can't continue mid-project?

It's covered by contract: I work in milestones with partial deliveries, so you always own what you've already paid for — source files included. Everything is documented in writing, so any professional could pick the project up without starting over. To date, it has never been needed.
