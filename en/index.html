Frequently asked questions

How much does a project cost?

Projects start at 500 USD for an initial branding or basic-website scope; most land between 500 and 2,500 USD depending on scope. Before we start you get a proposal with a fixed price, timeline and deliverables, with no surprises or hidden costs.

Do you work with clients outside Colombia?

Yes, I work 100% remotely with clients in several countries. Communication is written, over WhatsApp or email, so every agreement, update and delivery is documented. I'm in Bogotá (UTC-5) and I adapt to your time zone.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit to get started and the remaining 50% on delivery. Larger projects are split into milestones. For international clients I accept PayPal or Wise.

How many revisions are included?

It depends on the service, but generally I include two rounds of revisions within the agreed scope. If you need more, we price them separately, with a clear quote before any work.

Do you use contracts?

Always. Before starting we sign an agreement that defines the scope, timeline, price and delivery terms. It protects both of us and prevents misunderstandings down the road.

What if I need changes after delivery?

Minor changes within the original scope are free if you request them within the first 7 days. After that, or for major changes of direction, we price them separately, always with a clear quote first.

What time zone do you work in?

I'm in Bogotá, Colombia: UTC-5, no daylight saving. I usually reply between 8 a.m. and 8 p.m. Colombian time, and I'm flexible when we need to schedule a call across time zones.

What happens if you can't continue mid-project?

It's covered by contract: I work in milestones with partial deliveries, so you always own what you've already paid for, source files included. Everything is documented in writing so anyone could pick the project up without starting over. To date, it has never been needed.
